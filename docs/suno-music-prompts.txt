MUSIC FOR ARIANNA'S CUPCAKES: Suno prompts

For every track:
1. Custom mode. Turn Instrumental ON. Leave the lyrics empty.
2. Paste the Style text into "Style of Music".
3. Open Advanced Options and paste this into Exclude (the same for every track):
   vocals, singing, choir, humming, rap, spoken word
4. Paste the Title. Make two versions, keep the better one, download it as MP3.
5. Name the file with its code, like M2-shop.mp3, and send them over (a zip is perfect).

Full-length songs are fine. I'll cut each one down, make it loop smoothly, and play it quietly under the voices.
Prefer the version with a steady feel all the way through: no big build-ups, no sudden drops, no long fade at the start.
