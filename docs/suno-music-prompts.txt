MUSIC FOR ARIANNA'S CUPCAKES: Suno prompts

How to make each one:
1. In Suno, choose Custom mode and turn Instrumental ON (no singing; the game's voices need the space).
2. Paste the Style text into the style box and the Title into the title box. Leave lyrics empty.
3. Make two versions of each, keep the one you like best, and download it as MP3.
4. Name the file with its code, like M2-shop.mp3, and send them over (a zip is perfect).

Full-length songs are fine. I'll cut each one down, make it loop smoothly, and play it quietly under the voices.
Prefer the version with a steady feel all the way through: no big build-ups, no sudden drops, and no long fade at the start.
